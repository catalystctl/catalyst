## AuthZ (server access)

```
owner
OR ServerAccess contains required perm
OR role * | admin.write
OR (node assignment AND node.update)
```

Not sufficient: node assignment alone, admin.read alone.  
Ownership transfer ≠ `server.transfer` (node transfer).  
**Status:** Source-confirmed — `server-access.ts`, tests.
